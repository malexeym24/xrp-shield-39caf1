Method

How the numbers are made

Show the method and the sources
  • Same model as the main example, same snapshot: option prices from Deribit pulled , USDC rates read . All dollar figures use XRP at $2.00 and a 40% loan.
  • His XRP pledged: cost of USDC = middle of the 4 venues in the USDC table that accept XRP as collateral (), plus the same for custody, operations, reserve and losses and the same margin.
  • Our own money is counted at what it would earn in 3-month US Treasury bills: .
  • Exchange lines from the exchanges' own pages and public loan data, checked 5 Nov 2026: Binance borrow up to 78%, margin call 85%, sale 91%; Bybit 80%, 87%, 95%; Morpho cbXRP sale at 62.5%; OKX has no fixed line (margin account, XRP counted at 97%). Which price each exchange uses to decide a sale was not checked.
  • 10 Oct 2025: lowest 1-minute price on Binance, Coinbase and Bitstamp against 20:30 UTC that evening. A fall of the same size is applied to $2.00.
  • Buffer: the exchange loan is cut until its sale line sits at the chosen fall; the rest of the $8,000 is our own money at the T-bill rate.
  • Rate moves: Binance flexible USDC loan rate over the 90 days to ; Bybit fixed-rate loans read 5 Nov 2026. Our margin = his base rate minus the exchange rate minus running costs.
  • Exchange penalties on a sale, withdrawal fees and taxes are not included.
