Design principle
The problem

XRP at $2.00, a 40% loan, and a crash to $1.00

You hold 10,000 XRP at $2.00 ($20,000) and borrow 40%: $8,000 USDC. Lenders liquidate when the loan reaches a fixed share of the collateral value: 62.5% to 83% at dedicated lenders and DeFi markets, 91% to 95% on exchange loan desks that start you at 76% to 80%. With a round 80% trigger, this loan is liquidated at $1.00: a 50% drop, smaller than the 55% one-day wick XRP printed on 10 October 2025. Move the slider to see what happens with and without Shield.

How it works

Four steps, no forced sale in a crash

Shield is a put option, the right to sell XRP at a fixed price. We buy it for your loan every month and keep it on our books. You never trade options yourself.

Deposit XRP

Your XRP goes into a segregated wallet in your name. It is never lent out, staked or used to fund anything else.

Get USDC

Choose 20% to 50% LTV and receive USDC (or RLUSD). Interest accrues monthly; repay any time.

Shield is bought

Each month we buy a put on your XRP at your Shield level, the price where your loan would hit 75% LTV. The cost is in your rate.

Crash? You keep XRP

If XRP falls below the Shield level, the option pays out in USDC and that money pays down your loan. Your XRP stays yours.

Wick-proof price engine

We never act on one exchange's print. Our price is the median of several exchanges, and any action uses the 60-minute average of that median. A ten-minute wick on one venue changes nothing.

Slow declines: you are asked, not sold

If XRP drifts down and your LTV passes 55%, we ask you to add XRP or USDC to keep Shield on. Alerts at 55%, 60% and 65%, one-tap top-up, optional auto top-up from your My Neobank balance.

Upside stays yours

Crash Shield does not cap your gains. If XRP goes to $5, the option simply expires unused and your loan becomes an even smaller share of your collateral.

Crash test

Replay: the night of 10 October 2025

Between 20:30 and 21:30 UTC XRP collapsed and rebounded within hours. One-minute prices from three exchanges show how much the trigger price matters. Binance, Bybit and OKX all printed lows 55% to 57% below the day's open, while loans on those exchanges start with only a 14% to 18% cushion to liquidation. Take a 40% LTV loan opened at the 20:30 price: measured on a single exchange's price with an 80% trigger, it would have been liquidated. Our 60-minute multi-exchange average fell only 14.6%, so the loan never left the healthy zone, before Shield was even needed.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is any of my XRP sold to pay for Shield?

No. Shield is paid from the loan rate in USDC. Your XRP stays in a segregated wallet and is not lent out.

What exactly happens in a flash crash?

Our price engine ignores single-exchange wicks. If the move is real and XRP falls below your Shield level, the option's value counts as part of your collateral, so your LTV stays at or below 75% and nothing is sold. At expiry, whatever the option pays reduces your loan.

And if XRP falls slowly over months?

That is the one risk an affordable monthly shield does not remove. When your LTV passes 55% we ask you to add XRP or USDC so the next month's Shield can be bought. If you do nothing and the 60-minute price later passes 80% LTV, only the amount needed to restore a safe LTV is sold, never everything.

Why not guarantee no liquidation for the whole year?

You can, with Hard Floor, but at today's XRP option prices it costs about a year on a 40% LTV loan, because it insures every bear market, not only crashes. At 20 to 25% LTV it becomes reasonable.

Does Shield limit my upside?

Crash Shield and Hard Floor do not. Only the Zero-cost Collar does, because selling upside is what makes it free.

Where do the options come from?

Listed XRP options on the deepest venue today (Deribit, USDC-settled, expiries up to about two months), bought by our hedging desk for the whole loan book, plus longer-dated exchange or OTC options where available.

Why USDC?

It is the stablecoin most clients ask for. RLUSD on the XRP Ledger is an alternative for clients who want to stay inside the XRP ecosystem.
