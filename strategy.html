Strategy & economics

XRP Shield Loan

A USDC credit line for high-net-worth XRP holders who want liquidity without selling. The product's edge is not the rate, it is that a crash does not take the client's XRP. This page sets out the market today, the protection design, what it costs at live option prices and how to build it.

Defence in depth, not one expensive guarantee

Two different risks hide behind the word "liquidation". A flash crash (minutes to hours, then a rebound) hurts only because a lender sells at the bottom. A bear market (months) is a real loss of collateral value that no cheap instrument removes. The design treats them separately.

Why options and not insurance or a short hedge? A bought put needs no margin and cannot be liquidated, so it still works in the exact minutes a short perpetual hedge fails. It is exchange-traded and USDC-settled, so the payout is cash we can apply to the loan immediately. And it is priced every second by the market, so the protection cost is transparent and re-priced monthly.

What the market tells us

  • Cheap money exists (4 to 7% on exchange desks) but with a 14 to 18% cushion: the 10 Oct 2025 wick was 55 to 57% on those same exchanges.
  • Deep-cushion lenders charge 8 to 17% and still liquidate on price.
  • Nobody sells protection against the wick itself, except Nexo's collar, which settles the loan with the client's XRP.

Closest benchmarks for protection

  • Figure Liquidation Protection (BTC/ETH, US): no price liquidation for 12 months for 2% of the loan upfront S.
  • Binance Lite Loan (BTC, up to 1,000 USDT): no price liquidation for 30 days, 0.5 to 1% fee.
  • Nexo Zero-interest Credit: free, but a collar on the client's coins.

Our positioning

Price the loan in the dedicated-lender range (roughly 11 to 13% all-in with Crash Shield included at 30 to 40% LTV) and sell the one thing nobody offers: a crash does not take your XRP, and your upside is not capped.

5. Crash Shield mechanics

One formula, one rule

Strike and payout

Shield level K = Loan / (XRP pledged x 75%). Each month the desk buys puts on the pledged XRP at the listed strike at or just above K. While the put is live, the liquidation test uses the protected value = XRP value + option value, which can never be below XRP x K. So LTV on the protected value stays at or below 75%, under the 80% trigger, however far XRP falls inside the month.

At expiry the option pays XRP x max(K - price, 0) in USDC. That payout is credited to the loan principal. The client keeps every XRP. Next month the Shield is re-bought at the new K if LTV is at or below 55%; above that, the client is asked to top up first.

6. Cost model

What protection costs at today's prices

Monte Carlo, 20,000 one-year paths per LTV. Options priced with Black-Scholes on today's Deribit volatility smile (monthly expiry for Crash Shield, longest expiry for 2-month rolls), plus 25% for bid-ask, never below one price tick. XRP moves with annual volatility (365-day realized: ). Stress case: 80% volatility and option IV +15 points. Strikes more than 60% below spot (deeper than any recorded wick) are left to the Reserve. 8. Implementation

How it runs

Hedging playbook

  • Aggregate, then hedge. Each loan produces a strike K and an XRP amount. The desk rounds K up to the next listed strike and sums amounts per strike, in 1,000 XRP contracts.
  • Roll calendar. Monthly expiry (08:00 UTC, last Friday). Buy the next month two to three days before expiry; never leave a loan unprotected over a weekend.
  • Execution. Limit orders at mark, block trades through Paradigm above 75,000 XRP, sliced across hours. Ask-side market orders only in emergencies.
  • Counterparty. Bought options need only the premium: no margin, nothing to liquidate on our side. Cap exposure per venue; add CME or OTC as second venue once volume allows.
  • Re-price monthly. Shield fee is reset each month inside a published band; spikes above the band are absorbed by the Reserve, not the client.

Price engine and liquidation rules

  • Index = median of at least three exchanges' 1-minute prices; a venue more than 3% away from the others is dropped; stale feeds freeze actions, never trigger them.
  • Every decision uses the 60-minute average of the index, not the last print.
  • 55% LTV: top-up request, Shield not renewed until resolved. 60% and 65%: alerts with a 24-hour cure window.
  • 80% LTV on the protected value: sell only enough XRP to return to 65%, in slices across venues.
  • Option payouts at expiry are applied to principal the same day; the client sees it in the app.
