With Hard Floor the client is guaranteed: no sale for 12 months, same number of XRP, and below the insurance level every loss is paid. The client still loses value from $2.00 down to that level, like any holder. More XRP only if the client chooses to use the payout to buy XRP instead of repaying the loan.

How the insurance cost is calculated

We price put options with the standard Black-Scholes formula on the real Deribit option prices of , add 25% for the bid-ask spread and never go below Deribit's minimum price step. Then we simulate 20,000 possible years of XRP with its real volatility ( over the last 12 months) and apply the plan's rules every month: buy insurance, collect payouts, ask for top-ups. The cost shown is the average over those 20,000 years.

Why Hard Floor is expensive: two months of floor cost about of the loan while XRP is at $2.00, but after every fall the next two months are bought much closer to the price and cost far more. XRP has fallen 50% or more at some point in of 12-month periods since 2024.

Why Crash Shield is cheap: it insures one month at a time and only while the loan is at or below 55%; slow falls are handled by top-ups, not by insurance.

Buying protection

Where we can buy XRP insurance (checked 30 Oct 2026)

Short version: monthly on Deribit works today. Longer protection exists only as a dealer quote and costs about the same per year.

Deribit Use

The deepest XRP options market: open interest , settled in USDC, 1,000 XRP per contract. Longest expiry , so we buy monthly. Blocks from 75,000 XRP via Paradigm.

CME Paper only

XRP options are listed out to March 2028, but on 29 Oct 2026 the whole complex had 13 open contracts, no trades all week. Block minimum 250,000 XRP, needs a futures broker.

XRP ETF options No buyers

Chains on XRPC and XRPZ run to June 2027, but 2027 puts have no bids and placeholder asks, open interest 0 to 35 contracts.

OTC dealers Ask for quotes

Wintermute, STS Digital, Flowdesk and FalconX trade bespoke crypto options; Galaxy offers "collar loans". None publishes XRP terms: tenor, size and price only by request, under a master agreement.

Is one 12-month put cheaper than rolling? No. By the same option maths, one 12-month put at a 40% loan costs about 11 to 18% of the loan (volatility 70 to 85%), the same range as rolling Hard Floor (). It buys a fixed price, not a lower one. Worth asking dealers once the book is large.

How it runs

Hedging playbook

  • Aggregate, then hedge. Each loan produces a strike K and an XRP amount. The desk rounds K up to the next listed strike and sums amounts per strike, in 1,000 XRP contracts.
  • Roll calendar. Monthly expiry (08:00 UTC, last Friday). Buy the next month two to three days before expiry; never leave a loan unprotected over a weekend.
  • Execution. Limit orders at mark, block trades through Paradigm above 75,000 XRP, sliced across hours. Ask-side market orders only in emergencies.
  • Counterparty. Bought options need only the premium: no margin, nothing to liquidate on our side. Cap exposure per venue; add CME or OTC as second venue once volume allows.
  • Re-price monthly. Shield fee is reset each month inside a published band; spikes above the band are absorbed by the Reserve, not the client.

Price engine and liquidation rules

  • Index = median of at least three exchanges' 1-minute prices; a venue more than 3% away from the others is dropped; stale feeds freeze actions, never trigger them.
  • Every decision uses the 60-minute average of the index, not the last print.
  • 55% LTV: top-up request, Shield not renewed until resolved. 60% and 65%: alerts with a 24-hour cure window.
  • 80% LTV on the protected value: sell only enough XRP to return to 65%, in slices across venues.
  • Option payouts at expiry are applied to principal the same day; the client sees it in the app.

Risks

What can go wrong

  • Options get expensive after a crash: the monthly price is reset inside a published band; a reserve absorbs spikes.
  • One venue holds most XRP options: exposure limits, CME and OTC as backups.
  • Only short expiries: we roll every month; a missed roll is the main operational risk.
  • Slow bear market: Crash Shield does not cover it; clients must top up. Said upfront.
  • Price feed failure: bad data freezes actions, it never triggers a sale.
  • Model risk: real XRP jumps more than the model; we compare real hedge cost to the model every month.
